Capsicum Latin Fallback
=======================

CapsicumLatinFallback.ttf is a subset of DejaVu Sans (Book) containing only the
ASCII glyphs U+0020 (space), U+0023 (#), U+002A (*) and U+0030-U+0039 (0-9).
It is bundled solely to reclaim those code points from `Noto Color Emoji` on
Linux, where the color-emoji fallback otherwise steals their metrics and breaks
digit width (capsicum issue #869 / #871).

The font was renamed from "DejaVu Sans" to "Capsicum Latin Fallback" as required
by the Bitstream Vera license (modified fonts must not carry the "Bitstream" or
"Vera" names). No glyph outlines were altered.

Original font: DejaVu fonts — https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/
Source: /usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSans.ttf (fonts-dejavu-core)
